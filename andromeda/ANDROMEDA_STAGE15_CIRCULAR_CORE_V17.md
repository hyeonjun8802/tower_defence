# Andromeda Stage 15 Circular Core v17

## Purpose
The previous Stage 15 route looked like a large angular path with an oversized center core.
This patch makes the final boss stage read as a simple circular vortex converging into a small center core.

## Changes
- Stage 15 route now uses a generated circular spiral around the center core.
- The path starts outside the board, circles inward, and converges into the compact center core.
- The core visual radius was reduced so the route, not the core, provides the scale.
- Applied to Andromeda Stage 15 only. Legacy/Milky routes are not changed.
